I design for scale from day one, but I've spent just as much time on the harder problem: modernizing legacy PHP systems into Laravel without breaking production.

I lead by writing the standard, not just enforcing it — defining coding conventions, running architectural reviews, and mentoring engineers through structured code walkthroughs.
